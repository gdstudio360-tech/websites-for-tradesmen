FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to already own a domain?+

No. If you do not have one yet, we can help you choose and connect a suitable business domain.

Can I use photos from my phone?+

Yes. Real job photos are usually more useful than generic stock images. Good phone photos can be prepared for the website.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?+

Yes. We can include direct call and WhatsApp buttons so mobile visitors can contact you quickly.

Will I be able to change things later?+

Yes. You can request future changes when needed, or use the optional care plan for regular small updates.

Are there any extra costs?+

Your selected package covers the website work listed above. Domain registration, paid third-party services and additional work outside the agreed package are quoted separately before you approve them.

How many revisions are included?+

Starter includes 1 revision round, Business includes 2, and Pro includes 3. Additional revisions can be quoted if needed.

Do you guarantee more customers?

No website can honestly guarantee enquiries or sales. The goal is to give your business a professional online presence and make it easier for potential customers to understand your services and contact you.
