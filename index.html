WEBSITE CARE

You own the website. We look after the technical side.

Website Care is optional and is available for websites built by GD Studio 360. Each website package has one matching care level, so you can choose the website on its own or add ongoing maintenance after launch without having to manage the technical side yourself.

WHAT CARE COVERS Ongoing support for the GD Studio 360 website you choose above.

Starter Care includes routine health checks, backups and up to 30 minutes of small text or image updates each month. Business Care adds form and email checks, troubleshooting and up to 60 minutes of content updates. Pro Care adds support for the existing backend, automated emails, webhooks and payment integrations, with up to 90 minutes of maintenance. New pages, redesigns, new features, new integrations and paid third-party services are quoted separately.

ALREADY HAVE A WEBSITE? Our fixed-price Care plans are not for third-party-built websites.

If your current website was built elsewhere, we will not take over its ongoing maintenance under these plans. You can still contact us if you want a new GD Studio 360 website built in a setup we can properly support.

HOW IT WORKS

A clear process from enquiry to launch.

You keep running your business. We guide the project through each step, from the first enquiry to the finished website going live.

  1. 01
    Send your enquiry

    Choose Starter, Business or Pro — or select “Not sure” and we’ll recommend the most suitable option. Tell us the basics about your business and what you want the website to achieve.

  2. 02
    We review & confirm the project

    We review your enquiry and confirm the package, project scope, price, included revision rounds and any additional work before you commit.

  3. 03
    Approve & pay the deposit

    If you are happy to proceed, approve the project and pay the secure deposit through SumUp. Once the deposit is confirmed, the project can move into the build stage.

  4. 04
    Send us your content

    After the deposit is received, you automatically receive a private Content Portal link. Use it to send your website text, logo, photos, reviews and other project information. You can save your progress and return to the same private link later.

  5. 05
    We build, preview & refine

    We build the website using the agreed scope and the content you provide. You receive a preview to review, and we make the revision rounds included with your selected package.

  6. 06
    Launch, final payment & completion

    Once you approve the finished website, we connect it to your business domain and put it live. After launch, we send the secure SumUp link for the remaining balance. Once the final payment is received, the project is complete. If you selected Website Care, ongoing support continues after launch.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to already own a domain?+

No. If you do not have one yet, we can help you choose and connect a suitable business domain.

Can I use photos from my phone?+

Yes. Real photos of your work, team, premises or results are usually more useful than generic stock images. Good phone photos can be prepared for the website.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?+

Yes. We can include WhatsApp and enquiry buttons so mobile visitors can message you quickly.

Will I be able to change things later?+

Yes. You can request one-off changes when needed, or choose a Website Care plan for ongoing maintenance and included small updates after launch.

Do I have to take a Website Care plan?+

No. Website Care is optional. You still own the website. If you choose Care, GD Studio 360 continues handling the agreed maintenance and technical support for the website we built after launch.

Can you maintain a website built by someone else?+

Not under our fixed-price Website Care plans. Those plans are for websites built by GD Studio 360, where we know the code, hosting and integrations. If your current site was built elsewhere, we can instead discuss building a new site that we can properly support.

Are there any extra costs?+

Your selected package covers the website work listed above. Domain registration, paid third-party services and additional work outside the agreed package are quoted separately before you approve them.

How many revisions are included?+

Starter includes 1 revision round, Business includes 2, and Pro includes 3. Additional revisions can be quoted if needed.

Do you guarantee more customers?

No website can honestly guarantee enquiries or sales. The goal is to give your business a professional online presence and make it easier for potential customers to understand your services and contact you.
