Website & Enquiry Terms

These terms explain the website enquiry process and the standard project workflow if GD Studio 360 accepts your project and you choose to proceed.

No purchase is created by submitting the form.

Sending an enquiry does not create a paid contract, commit you to buy a website, or require GD Studio 360 to accept a project.

If both sides decide to proceed, GD Studio 360 will confirm the selected package or agreed scope, project total, deposit and any important project details before paid work starts.

You are responsible for providing reasonably accurate information about your business, services, relevant qualifications, location or service area, prices and contact details.

You should not ask us to publish claims that are false, misleading or unlawful. We may ask for clarification or evidence before using claims about qualifications, memberships, guarantees, reviews or similar trust information.

If you provide photos, logos, text, reviews, graphics or other material, you confirm that you own it, have permission to use it, or otherwise have the right to provide it for use on your website.

You remain responsible for obtaining any permissions needed for customer photos, testimonials, trademarks or third-party content that you supply.

5. Pricing shown on the website

Package prices shown on the website describe the standard scope of those packages. The final price for a project may change where you request work outside the package scope, paid third-party services, extra pages, extra revisions, specialist functionality or other additions.

Any additional cost will be discussed before that additional work is carried out. Submitting an enquiry by itself does not authorise a charge.

6. Timings

Any timescale discussed at enquiry stage is an estimate unless a specific delivery date is later agreed. Project timing may depend on how quickly you provide content, approvals, feedback, access details and other information needed to complete the work.

Search-engine rankings and third-party platforms are outside our direct control and can change over time.

Any paid third-party service that you are expected to pay for will be identified before it is added to the agreed project.

11. Privacy

Personal information submitted through the form is handled as described in our Privacy Notice.

12. If a paid project goes ahead

Submitting an enquiry is not an order. If GD Studio 360 accepts the project, we may send a written project approval confirming the selected package or agreed scope, project total, deposit and any approved additions.

If you choose to proceed and pay the requested deposit, the written project approval, agreed package or scope and these Website & Enquiry Terms form the basis of the standard project arrangement unless something different has been agreed in writing.

Important

Nothing in these terms removes any rights or protections that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

13. Project approval and deposit

For standard Starter, Business and Pro projects, the normal deposit is 50% of the agreed project total.

The agreed project total may include approved additions and can therefore differ from the standard package price shown on the website.

Paid project work starts after the project has been accepted, the relevant terms have been agreed and the required deposit has been received.

Any cancellation or refund arrangements will be considered according to the circumstances, the work already completed, any written project agreement and applicable law.

14. Content Portal and customer material

After the deposit is received, GD Studio 360 may send you a private Content Portal link. The portal can be used to provide website text, logos, photographs, reviews, service information, contact details and other material needed for the project.

You may save your progress and return to the same private project link later. You are responsible for ensuring that material you provide is accurate and that you have the necessary rights or permissions to use it.

15. Build, preview and revisions

GD Studio 360 builds the website using the agreed scope and the material supplied for the project. A website preview is provided before launch so you can review the work and request the revisions included with your package.

Starter includes 1 revision round, Business includes 2 revision rounds and Pro includes 3 revision rounds unless something different has been agreed in writing.

Additional revisions, new pages, new functionality or other work outside the agreed scope may be quoted separately before that additional work is carried out.

16. Launch and final payment

Once the website has been reviewed and approved, and the required domain, hosting or account access is available, GD Studio 360 connects the website to the agreed business domain and launches it.

After the website is live, GD Studio 360 sends the secure SumUp payment link for the remaining project balance.

Once the remaining balance has been received, the project is marked complete.

17. Website Care plans

Website Care is optional and is available for websites built by GD Studio 360. Each website package has one matching care level: Starter Care, Business Care or Pro Care.

Care is activated separately after launch. Monthly billing and any applicable ongoing cancellation or notice arrangements are confirmed when the care plan is activated.

The fixed-price care plans cover the routine maintenance and small-update allowance shown on the website.

Websites built by other providers, unfamiliar third-party code, hosting environments, plugins or integrations are not included in these fixed-price plans unless GD Studio 360 expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

Care plans do not include unlimited development. New pages, redesigns, new functionality, new integrations, advertising services and paid third-party services are quoted separately. Unused monthly update time does not roll over.

The website remains the customer's website. Ending a care plan does not by itself transfer ownership away from the customer.

18. Changes to these terms

We may update these Website & Enquiry Terms for future enquiries and projects. The version that applies is the version presented when the relevant project arrangement is made.
